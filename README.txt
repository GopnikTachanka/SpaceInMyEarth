Hi judges! We are Steel Team, based in Monclova, Mexico. We hope you like our page and take us into consideration!
We chose the "Identify Earth Locations that Analog the Permanent Moon Base Locations and Mars" challenge,
and our page is just about that.
We decided to go for an interactive map, where the user can go through several analogous sites here on Earth,
some already confirmed or tested by NASA and other space agencies, while others are hypotheses based on data.
SI was used for most of the JavaScript file, as this was quite literally our first time using the
language, and for the most complex parts of the CSS, as well as for data compilation using the given resources as
criteria and sources.
Nevertheless, we tried do as most of the idea as we could by hand or searching through free forums like
Stack Overflow and GitHub.
